## Start here

1. [Get started](GETTING_STARTED.md): build or launch the app, practice with the simulator, and collect your first training visit.
2. [Use Training Studio](MAC_APP.md): recording controls, optional permissions, calibration, paired scripts, review, and whole-visit baseline training.
3. [Choose an enclosure](ENCLOSURES.md) and follow [hardware bring-up](HARDWARE.md) when your radar is ready.

The installed app is training-focused: transcription, text insertion, and radar predictions remain disabled. Hardware and speech performance still require physical validation. Recordings, reference media, trained models, purchase records, downloaded vendor packages, and viewer logs are excluded from the public repository.
