# Capture enclosure hardware — v1.2

This is a separate enclosure for **IWR6843AOPEVM Rev G + MMWAVEICBOOST Rev B + DCA1000EVM Rev A**. The original pill enclosure remains a separate build. Counts below cover one complete capture-stack enclosure.

| Hardware | Used here | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| M3 × 25 socket-cap screws | 4 | DCA1000 to rear-shell standoffs |
| M3 × 16 socket-cap screws | 8 | Four screws through the ripple cover and front into the rear shell, two pivots, two angle locks |
| M3 × 12 socket-cap screws | 6 | Four ICBOOST mounts, two rear cable-guide screws |
| Standard M3 hex nuts | 18 | All M3 fasteners; captive pockets |
| M3 flat washers | 10 | Four shell screws, four stand screws, two cable-guide screws |
| M2 × 6 low-head Phillips screws | 2 | Radar board to front standoffs |
| M2 brass heat-set inserts, **3.2 mm OD × 4 mm long** | 2 | Radar mounting standoffs |
| 150 × 2.5 mm cable ties | Up to 6 | USB/Ethernet/power strain relief and rear routing |

Also print **one removable cable guide and one ripple cover**. Compared with v1.1, the four outer closure screws change from M3 × 12 to **M3 × 16** to accommodate the cover. The four ICBOOST screws remain M3 × 12. No additional fastener type is introduced in v1.2.

Print **four 1.8 mm DCA washers, four 0.8 mm ICBOOST washers, and two 0.6 mm AOP washers**. Their thicknesses are part of the fastener-length design. Do not replace them with thinner metal washers without checking screw protrusion.

M2.5 screws/inserts and ¼″-20 nuts used by the standalone enclosure are not needed in this tabletop enclosure. No new standoffs, hinges, locknuts, adhesive, or feet are required. Standard metal nuts have modeled nominal dimensions of 5.5 mm across flats and 2.4 mm thickness; pockets allow 5.8 mm across flats and 2.8 mm depth. Check a nut in the supplied coupon before printing the large parts.

**Example component references:** [M3 assortment](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GHMM79M9), [M2 screws](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B082XXW76M), [heat-set inserts](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CLKDPN65), [2.5 mm ties](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DR8KSVQD).

Capture electronics and their operating cables are separate: use the DCA1000's supplied short Samtec LVDS cable and the power, USB and Ethernet connections required by TI. The enclosure is designed around that supplied 50.8 mm cable; no longer replacement is specified. The supplied cable's actual connector exit shape and bend clearance still need a bench fit check.
